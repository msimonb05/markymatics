## The project

Sneakslab is an online sneaker store built for Web Systems and Technologies 2 at the University of Baguio. Shoppers can browse a 366-sneaker catalog, filter by brand, price and size, see prices in their local currency, keep a wishlist, check out with saved addresses and track their orders.

## What I did

In a team of two, I handled the back end and quality assurance. The back end runs on Node.js with Express 5 and MongoDB. Accounts use sessions with bcrypt-hashed passwords, and verification and password-reset emails are sent through Resend. An admin back office covers orders, sales, stock, users, support tickets and activity logs, and a live support chat runs over Socket.IO with one room per ticket. I tested the store before its deployment on Render.
