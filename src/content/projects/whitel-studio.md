## The problem

WhiteL Studio, an independent animation and game studio in the Philippines with more than 15 people, needed a portfolio site that its staff could update without a developer.

## What I am building

I lead development of a MERN-stack portfolio and content management system and own its back-end architecture. Firebase Authentication handles sign-in, and Owner and Editor roles let authorized staff publish on their own. Media files are stored in Cloudflare R2. The CMS includes modules for projects, team members and job postings, and project credits link automatically to the people listed.
