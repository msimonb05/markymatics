## The project

Kepler Blog was my first website. It gathers space and Earth science topics in one place, with articles on dark matter, merged galaxies, cosmic radiation, warming oceans and the 2024 total solar eclipse over North America.

## What I did

I designed and coded every page by hand. The home page features the latest articles, and the service pages include a daily NASA image and fact based on NASA's Images API and an interactive map of global temperature and wind. The site also has about and contact pages and a responsive navigation menu, and it is deployed on Netlify.
