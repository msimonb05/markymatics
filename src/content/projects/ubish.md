## The problem

Barangay Greenwater Village in Baguio City handled clearances, incident reports and public disclosures on paper for a community of more than 1,500 residents.

## What I did

I led a four-member team through a three-month build and wrote the back end for every module except announcements:

- Staff accounts, with registration approved by an administrator, login, and password recovery through security questions
- Employee records, plus resident and household records with demographics
- Incident reports
- Certificate and clearance requests from residents, along with document and permit management

The application runs on PHP with a 17-table MariaDB database, with separate areas for administrators and barangay staff.

## Outcome

The platform brings the barangay's clearances, incident reports and records into one system for its residents.
